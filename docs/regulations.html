Item Pool

  • Items from the newest update are the main focus.
  • Older items on the legal-items list may also be used.

Restricted Rules

  • Each player may equip a maximum of one restricted item.
  • Banned items cannot be used.
  • Players may change legal items between games.

Match Rules

  • Matches are best of three.
  • Only approved arenas may be used.
  • Both players must agree before the match starts.
  • If a rule violation affects the game, that game point may go to the other player after staff review.

Regulation Codes

A regulation code has two parts: the first letter selects the item pool and the second letter selects the rule modifier.

Rule Modifier: Rules
